# Català synonym exercises

Questions q301–q330 add 20 intruder exercises (including one non-equivalent pair)
and 10 equivalent-expression exercises. The original q001–q300 are unchanged.
All choices are in Catalan; French feedback explains the family and the contrast.
Sentences and paraphrases are authored for the exercise, not presented as verbatim
quotations. The IDs below anchor each topic in `src/data/questions.json`.

Synonymy is contextual: stopping verbs do not merge legal definitions of stopping
and parking; starting an engine differs from moving off; reversing differs from a
U-turn; a lane is part of a carriageway. `Vorera d’emergència` is treated as a whole
expression, distinct from ordinary `vorera`.

Terminology checked against the Gran Diccionari de la llengua catalana:
[vorera](https://www.diccionari.cat/GDLC/vorera),
[vorada](https://www.diccionari.cat/GDLC/vorada),
[arrencar](https://www.diccionari.cat/GDLC/arrencar),
[engegar](https://www.diccionari.cat/GDLC/engegar),
[minvar](https://www.diccionari.cat/GDLC/minvar),
[girar](https://www.diccionari.cat/GDLC/girar), and
[superar](https://www.diccionari.cat/GDLC/superar).
